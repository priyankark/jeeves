# Jeeves — visual and verbal notes

## Direction

Editorial, assured, and useful. The campaign uses the existing abstract Jeeves mark and the visual restraint of the established omnibus-cover direction: solid colour, strong typography, clear geometry, generous space. Avoid literal butlers, top hats, monocles, service bells, cartoon assistants, and ornamental “British” jokes.

**Campaign line:** Good work. In good order.

**Product descriptor:** Local-first workflows for your AI agents.

**Secondary line:** A little direction. A lot done.

## Colour

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Burnt orange / campaign accent | `#E76632` |
| Ink / primary text | `#202126` |
| Warm paper / base | `#F7F2E8` |

| Muted text | `#625F57` |
| Olive / supporting product accent | `#506440` |

Use ink on orange for copy. Small white text on orange is not part of this system. Keep gradients and decorative shadows out of campaign art; the product screenshots may retain the application's actual styling.

## Type

Manrope for headlines and the lowercase wordmark. DM Sans for body text. Font files are bundled for offline use, with their licenses in `assets/`. Monospace appears only for filenames and package structure.

Headlines use tight tracking, short lines, and generous surrounding space. Body copy uses clear sentences and concrete verbs: choose, connect, review, run, schedule, export.

## Mark

`assets/jeeves-icon.png` is copied unchanged from the product's existing icon. Do not redraw or recolour it. Preserve its square proportions and leave clear space of approximately one-quarter of its width. The wordmark lockup is editable HTML typography, not a newly registered logotype.

The campaign's connecting-line illustration is original SVG geometry written in `tools/build.mjs`. It describes workflow structure; it is not a product screenshot or a replacement app icon. No new AI-generated bitmap asset was required for this kit.

## Screenshots

Fresh screenshots were captured from the production UI in a separate temporary workspace with synthetic example workflows and no provider credentials. They are labelled as Demo workspace imagery. Screenshots are presented inside layouts without changing the product pixels. Preserve mode indicators and avoid cropping away context that would misrepresent a simulated result as live.
