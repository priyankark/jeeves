A few useful details

What does local-first mean here?

Workflows, chats, and run history are stored on your computer. Live cloud providers receive the context needed for their steps. Local storage does not mean every model call stays on your machine.

Do I need another account?

Jeeves has no account system. Live providers and external services may require their own credentials. Demo mode lets you explore with simulated model responses.

Can I schedule work while the app is closed?

The local engine must be running for scheduled work. Schedules can resume after sleep according to their configuration; this is not an always-on hosted service.

Can I try it today?

This is a product preview. The current desktop build has been tested on macOS Apple Silicon and is unsigned. Public downloads and public source access have not been announced.
