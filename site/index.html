A few particulars.

Is this a finished product?

Jeeves is a desktop preview. Installers are available for Mac, Windows, and Linux; preview builds are not signed with a trusted publisher certificate or notarized. Review the release notes before installing.

Does every workflow need Jev?

No. Jev belongs where the next step depends on a judgment: triage, readiness, evidence, or quality. A simple translation can stay linear. Exact comparisons use rules. Your approval stays yours.

Does local-first mean everything stays offline?

The workspace is local. Live cloud AI steps send task context to the service you connect. Browser tasks visit websites in a separate Chrome session. Scheduled runs need the local engine to be running.

Will it place orders or publish things for me?

The browser stops for manual review before checkout, payments, messages, or publishing. Other workflow integrations have their own configured actions, which you can inspect before running.
